Resume

After graduating from the University of Toronto with a degree in Medieval History and Classics I was standing on a bit of a threshold, conflicted on what I wanted to do next. I loved history and it provided me with an engaging vehicle to satisfy my interest and passion in learning about people, ideas and culture. I am a person with many curiosities and a lot of passion for learning new things. At that time I started to feel like my cup was a little full of academia and I was beginning to think about different avenues I could potentially pursue that would satisfy my passion and curiosity in recognizing patterns in human behavior.

After University I worked a lot of different odd jobs and bounced around from place to place trying to find something that fit, and make ends meet of course. It was around this time that a friend of mine gave me the opportunity to learn about working as a web analyst. My family ran a small UX design company contracting for large American corporations out of California. I was able to get the opportunity to work for my family company doing Web analytics at first, and I soon learned about the intersection of UX design and UX research. I found the process fascinating, and it reignited the spark in me to renew my search for a career that would allow me to use my research skills and curiosity in a way that was present and prosperous.
