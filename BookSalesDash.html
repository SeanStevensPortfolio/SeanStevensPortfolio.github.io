    • Book & Ebook Sales Dashboard

    A dashboard I created for my own personal study using PowerBI

    This was a dashboard I made on my own free time to keep my skills in visualizing and processing large data sets up to date. I found a large data set from Kaggle.com containing book and e-book sales from amazon.com which I then cleaned and transferred into PowerBI. The goal of this exercise was to utilize the powerful visualization capabilities of PowerBI to extract insights from this large market dataset. I approached this dashboard with the objective of creating visuals that were easy to view and understand and possessed a more intuitive and simple layout. I wanted to practice making clear visuals that effectively conveyed insights for stakeholders. The dashboard includes two pages: The first page provides insights on publisher sales data and the second page provides insights related to the authors sales data and performance.

    Left & Right

    The dashboard provides charts that showcase different publisher-related sales insights. I utilized bar charts to showcase information such as the number of units each publisher had sold, and their total revenue. I used pie charts to show the proportion of sales by publisher, which reinforced the picture of who the top selling publishers were. I also utilized an additional pie chart to provide some insight into the proportion of genre’s being sold – allowing the user to gain a more clear understanding of the top selling genres. I also utilized a treemap chart to showcase in a more clear and visually dynamic manner the average selling price for books by each publisher – enabling the user to see which publisher on average had a higher or lower price point for their books.
